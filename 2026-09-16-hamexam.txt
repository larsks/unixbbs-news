Subject: Amateur radio practice questions

You can now use the `hamexam` command to receive random questions from
the FCC question pool of your choice. For example:

    N1LKS$ hamexam general
    Question G1B10
    ===============

    What is the power limit for beacon stations?

    A. 10 watts PEP output
    B. 20 watts PEP output
    C. 100 watts PEP output
    D. 200 watts PEP output

    Your answer? c

    That's correct! Great work.

    N1LKS$ 

Run `hamexam -l` to see the list of available question pools.
